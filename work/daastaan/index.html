A note from the house   /   01

India is not
one flavour.

It is the pickle jar you weren’t allowed to open. The train-platform chai that tasted better because you were going somewhere. The Sunday lunch that began at noon and forgot to end.

We made Daastaan for all of it: the big, complicated, delicious map of the things we carry with us. Not a greatest-hits tour. Not a museum. A living conversation between the flavours we know and the ones we haven’t met yet.

        01 / THE PLATE

        A little of this.

        Black dal that has taken its time. Smoke-kissed aubergine. Greens with a little snap left in them. A mound of saffron rice, still letting off steam.

        02 / THE PAUSE

        A little of that.

        Burnt-lime pickle, a bright chutney, warm roti for the last impossible scrape of the bowl. Every bite finds its own way around the table.

        03 / THE POINT

        Pass it on.

        A thali is an invitation to notice how flavours change beside each other. No one version. No correct first bite. Just room for another story.
